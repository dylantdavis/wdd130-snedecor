Discover the Thrill Below the Surface

Ready to explore the world beneath your feet? Our site brings the basics of rappelling and spelunking to life with interactive guides, clear visuals, and step-by-step lessons on essential harness systems, knots, and safety techniques. Whether you're a first-time adventurer or brushing up on your skills, you'll find everything you need to descend confidently and explore responsibly. Learn how to prepare, protect, and respect the underground environment—then discover some of the most breathtaking caves waiting to be explored.

Gear

Ascension

In the process of climbing up a fixed rope, specialized devices are used that grip the rope in one direction but slide freely in the other.

Descension

In the process of controlled descent down a rope, a friction-creating device is used. The user holds one end of the rope and uses a "descender" device to control the rate of their movement down the rope.

Knots

When ascending and descending on a rope, particular knots are essential to know and use in order to safely explore.
